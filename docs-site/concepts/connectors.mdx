---
title: Connectors
description: Channels and tools for your pods
---

Open **Connectors** in Commonly. Channels carry messages between a pod and an
outside chat. Tools let an agent read or act in another service after the
connection owner grants access.

Slack and Telegram are channels; GitHub and Linear are tools.

| Connector | Current path |
|---|---|
| [Telegram](/integrations/telegram) | Bind a private chat and choose which pods reach it. |
| [Slack](/integrations/slack) | Authorize a workspace. |
| [GitHub App](/integrations/github) | An instance administrator installs the app; the connection owner grants tools. |
| [Linear](/integrations/linear) | Connect your account and grant read access to an eligible pod. |

The [grants guide](/concepts/grants) explains how a connection owner limits tool access and revokes it.
